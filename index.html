QuickBooks Online Integration

Boise First UCC — internal bookkeeping tool

This page describes a private application used by Boise First UCC to maintain its own accounting records in QuickBooks Online. It is not offered to, sold to, or installed by anyone outside our organization, and it is not listed in the QuickBooks App Store.

What it does

  • Records journal entries for donor-restricted contributions and the spending of restricted funds.
  • Creates vendor bills from invoices we receive, and attaches the invoice document to the bill.
  • Retrieves financial reports, such as the statement of activities and the balance sheet, for month-end review.

Every entry is reviewed by an authorized person before it is posted. The application does not delete records in QuickBooks.

How it is built and run

It is our own build of Intuit's open-source QuickBooks Online MCP server, run as a local program on a workstation we control. An AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude), operated by our own staff under our own account, calls the application's tools to draft the entries and reports described above. There is no public website, no hosted service, and no sign-up: the only user is the QuickBooks company administrator who connected the application.

Connecting and disconnecting

Because the application runs locally, there is no web page to sign in to. It is authorized once through Intuit's standard OAuth process by our company administrator.

To disconnect it from a QuickBooks Online company, sign in to QuickBooks Online and go to Settings → Apps → My Apps, then disconnect this application. Access ends immediately when the authorization is revoked.
