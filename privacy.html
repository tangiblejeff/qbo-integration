Privacy Policy

Boise First UCC QuickBooks Online integration · Last updated September 17, 2026

This policy explains how the private QuickBooks Online integration operated by Boise First UCC handles data. The integration serves only our own organization; it has no customers, no public users, and no sign-up.

Data we access

Using credentials our company administrator authorizes, the integration reads and writes accounting records in our own QuickBooks Online company: the chart of accounts, classes, vendors, journal entries, bills, attachments, and financial reports. It does not access QuickBooks payroll or payments data, and it does not delete records.

How the data is used

Solely to maintain and report the organization's own financial records: recording donor-restricted contributions and spending, creating vendor bills from invoices we receive, attaching those invoices to the corresponding bills, and producing month-end reports for internal review.

Where the data is held

The integration runs as a local program on a workstation the organization controls. It keeps no database of its own. QuickBooks data exists in QuickBooks Online and, transiently, in memory while a request is processed. Vendor invoices we choose to attach are read from local folders on that workstation. API credentials and the OAuth refresh token are stored in a local configuration file on that workstation, protected by operating-system account permissions, and are excluded from source control.

AI processing

The integration is operated through an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) under the organization's own account. Accounting data included in a request, such as report figures or invoice details, is sent to that provider to process the request, under the terms of our account with them. QuickBooks data is not used to train any model. No other third party receives QuickBooks data from us.

Retention

We retain no separate copy of QuickBooks data beyond the records in QuickBooks Online itself and the invoice documents kept with our own accounting files, which are retained according to the organization's record-retention practices. Credentials are kept only while the integration is authorized, and are deleted when it is disconnected.

Personal data

Accounting records may include names and contact details of vendors and donors. These are handled as part of the organization's financial records, are not sold or shared for marketing, and are not disclosed outside the organization except as required by law or as needed by our accountants or auditors.

Security

Access is limited to authorized personnel of the organization. The workstation uses account authentication, a screen lock, and full-disk encryption. Authorization can be revoked at any time in QuickBooks Online under Settings → Apps → My Apps.
